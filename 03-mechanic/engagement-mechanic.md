# The Mechanic · FinWise

> Module 3 · Retention & Engagement. The engagement mechanic that builds the habit loop, with rationale and a wireframe.

## The mechanic

**Mechanic:** Streak Counter

- **Trigger:** A personalized notification reminding the user to review their business's financial health.
- **Action:** The user opens FinWise, reviews their financial dashboard, and confirms their weekly financial check-in.  The dashboard highlights:  Cash inflow and outflow Upcoming payments Spending trends Opportunities to improve cash flow
- **Reward:** The user receives immediate feedback through an updated streak counter, along with insights into their business's financial performance.
- **Investment:** The user reviews the financial insights and takes a meaningful action, such as categorizing a transaction, setting a financial goal, or planning an upcoming payment.  This action helps improve the accuracy and usefulness of future financial recommendations.

_____

## Rationale

FinWise faces a retention challenge where users may not consistently experience enough ongoing value to justify continuing their subscription.

A Financial Health Streak encourages users to develop the habit of regularly reviewing their finances rather than visiting the app only when a problem arises.

The mechanic taps into two psychological motivations:

Progress: Users feel a sense of accomplishment when they consistently monitor their business's financial health.

Loss Aversion: Users may feel motivated to maintain their streak, although the design should avoid creating unnecessary pressure.

The objective is not simply to increase app visits but to help users recognize FinWise as a valuable tool for making better business decisions.

Hypothesis:

IF FinWise introduces a weekly Financial Health Streak that rewards users for reviewing their finances and taking meaningful actions,

THEN users will complete more weekly financial check-ins and demonstrate stronger retention,

BECAUSE consistent financial visibility helps small business owners recognize ongoing value in FinWise.

Measured With:

Weekly financial check-in completion rate
Percentage of users completing meaningful financial actions
Weekly active users
Paid subscriber retention rate
Churn rate

These metrics would help us determine whether increased engagement translates into meaningful customer value.
