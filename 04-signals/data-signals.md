## The data pattern

The headline metric is Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate, which remains stuck around 2%, but the real pattern is that increasing financial data import usage is not translating into higher paid conversions. This suggests that completing the initial setup alone may not be enough for users to experience FinWise's core value. The potential problem lies between importing financial data and experiencing meaningful financial insights that justify paying for the product.

_Metric patterns: Conversion, This looks like a ceiling because conversion consistently stays around 2% across 13 months. Despite fluctuations in engagement, there is no sustained improvement in paid conversion. Modeling usage, This looks like a cliff because usage drops sharply from 58.06% in September 2024 to 36.04% in October 2024. However, earlier fluctuations suggest inconsistent adoption rather than a sustained decline.. Import usage, This looks like a ceiling because usage fluctuates between approximately 30% and 45%, with no sustained growth beyond 45%. Adoption appears to have reached a plateau in recent months.. Session duration, This looks like a cliff because session duration drops sharply from 12.11 minutes in June 2024 to 6.99 minutes in July and 5.14 minutes in August. There is a slight recovery afterward.. Sessions/user, This doesn't fit the three patterns cleanly. Sessions declined from 9 in October 2023 to 3 in January 2024, but gradually recovered to 9 by September 2024. This suggests a recovery rather than a continuous slow leak.._

## Leading indicators

- **Percentage of trial users who successfully complete their first financial data import.:** Importing financial data is the essential first step toward generating a financial model and experiencing FinWise's value.
- **Percentage of users who generate their first financial model within 7 days of starting a trial.:** Faster time to first value may increase the likelihood of users adopting FinWise and eventually becoming paying customers.

_____

## Lagging indicators

- **Percentage of paid customers retained after 12 months.:** Higher 12-month retention would indicate that more paying customers continue finding enough value to keep their subscriptions.
